# 👨‍💼 Employee Performance & Attrition Analytics — Power BI

An HR analytics dashboard designed to understand **employee performance, compensation, overtime, satisfaction, tenure, and attrition risk**.

## 🎯 Business Problem

HR teams need to understand what factors are associated with employee attrition and where retention efforts should be prioritized.

This project combines Power BI modeling and DAX to transform employee-level data into actionable HR KPIs.

## 🧰 Tools

- **Power BI** — dashboard and data visualization
- **DAX** — calculated measures and Flight Risk Score
- **Data Analysis** — KPI and segment analysis

## 📊 Dataset

- 800 employees
- Performance and satisfaction indicators
- Compensation information
- Overtime status
- Tenure
- Attrition information

## 🔍 Dashboard Analysis

- Employee attrition rate
- Performance ratings
- Salary and compensation
- Overtime analysis
- Employee satisfaction
- Tenure distribution
- Department-level performance
- Flight Risk Score

## 🧮 DAX / Flight Risk Score

A custom **Flight Risk Score** combines employee indicators such as satisfaction, overtime, and tenure to help identify employees who may require closer retention attention.

## 💡 Key Insight

In the analyzed dataset, employees working overtime leave at approximately **1.5× the rate** of employees who do not work overtime.

This highlights overtime as an important factor to investigate alongside satisfaction, compensation, workload, and career progression.

## 🚀 Business Recommendations

- Review workload and overtime patterns in high-attrition teams.
- Investigate employees with high Flight Risk Scores.
- Combine compensation and satisfaction analysis before designing retention programs.
- Monitor attrition KPIs regularly by department and tenure group.
